# Third-party notices — ReviewGate desktop (Windows)

The desktop client ships these components for its terminal (terminal chat and the shell panel). Each is used under the
licence quoted below. The other dependencies (Electron, React, Monaco, …) carry their own notices inside the app.

| Component | Version | Licence | Shipped as |
|---|---|---|---|
| node-pty | 1.1.0 | MIT (three notices below) | `resources/app.asar.unpacked/node_modules/node-pty` |
| ConPTY (`conpty.dll`, `OpenConsole.exe`, from Microsoft Terminal) | 1.23.251008001 | MIT | inside node-pty's `prebuilds/win32-x64/conpty` |
| winpty (`winpty.dll`, `winpty-agent.exe`) | as bundled by node-pty 1.1.0 | MIT | inside node-pty's `prebuilds/win32-x64` (never used: ReviewGate always asks for ConPTY) |
